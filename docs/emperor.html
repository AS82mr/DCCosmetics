Java Engine & Multiplayer Scaling

EmperorBossManager

  • BossBar: Tracks helly in world lowercity every 10 ticks and displays a crimson NOTCHED_20 Adventure BossBar to players within 80.0m.
  • Difficulty Scaling: Solo = 1.0x; each additional Survival/Adventure player in 80m adds +0.33x outgoing boss damage and incoming damage dampening in EmperorListener.
  • Heal Displays: Renders rising emissive TextDisplay popups (+X ❤, tagged emp_heal_txt) with heartbeat audio on Void Holder pulses.
  • TAB v6.x Hook: EmperorTabHook feeds %dragoncote_emperor_*% and %dragoncote_active_events_*% directly into TAB.

Void Holders & Barrier Counters

Void Holder Tether (CRIMSON_MINION)

  • When helly is within 80 blocks, Void Holders land on shroomlight pads and fire green heal pulses (voidholder_heal_projectile).
  • 5-Hit Sever: VoidHolder_OnHit counts hits in Survival, Creative, OP, and left/right interact. Hit 5 triggers VoidHolder_Sever, lifting the minion into STASIS (12–13 blocks up) and running voidholders deactivate.
  • Phase heal caps pause heals at thresholds (<999 P1, <800 P2, <600 P3) and during barriers/stasis.

Mace/Spear Barrier & Counter Scrolls

  • Un-breached flying Mace smashes and Trident throws/melee are canceled unless Emperor has tag frozen_stasis or attacker holds mace_bypassed.
  • Use a Mace infused with Chrono-Slip Scroll (5th hit breaches), trigger Temporal Stasis Core (15s freeze), and stack Emperor Slayer Scrolls (+15% / +20% / +35%).
