System Layers

DCEssentials runs inside the DCCosmetics host plugin on Paper 26.2 + ProtocolLib and coordinates four core layers:

1. Core & Cosmetics Host

Plugin lifecycle, SQLite/MySQL v2 persistence, ProtocolLib packet rewriting for expanded tooltips & display entities, Profile GUI slots, and the 3D Wardrobe Camera Studio.

2. DCEItems & 16-Stat Engine

Reads PDC stats from 7 StatSource slots, enforces soft/hard caps in StatAggregator (cached 250ms), and executes combat mitigation & gathering bonuses via StatCombatEngine & GatheringBonusService.

3. Native Keystones & DCEPets

Native head keystones (Tesseract, Chronolith, Singularity) powered by a 1,000 mC Microcharge buffer, Overdraw Heat thermodynamics, and stationary-perched DCEPets with weighted Adapt SkillDomain XP.

4. Emperor, Events & DCNPCs

MythicMobs helly 5-phase Sovereign boss scaling via EmperorBossManager + TAB hooks, rotating tri-world hub cycles, and the 43-step Citizens NPC pipeline.
