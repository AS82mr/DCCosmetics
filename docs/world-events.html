Rotating World Cycles & Mini-Events (events.yml)

3-Realm Hub Cycle

  • Sanctuary Dawn (spawn_main · 180m): Prime sunlit hub world.
  • The Void Convergence (spawn_bw · 60m): Monochrome realm with crow flocks, dimensional rifts, and +25% shadow drops.
  • Glacial Frost Grand Prix (spawn_snow · 60m): Polar hub for IceBoat heats, Colosseum duels, and 1.5x snow agility.
  • Evacuation Safety: 60s countdown teleports hub players cleanly to lowercity before swap.

Scheduled Mini-Events

  • Colosseum King of the Hill (colosseum_koth): 20m every 120m · double PvP honor (/warp colosseum).
  • Adapt EXP Surge (adapt_exp_surge): 30m every 180m · global 2x Adapt skill XP.
  • Supersonic IceBoat Rally (iceboat_rally): 15m every 90m · drift circuit (/warp iceboat).

Canon NPC Pipeline, Fast Travel & Meters

Tri-World DCNPCs

/dcnpc canon live replicates Sector 1 NPCs across spawn_main, spawn_bw (grayscale skins), and spawn_snow (frost textures) while keeping Sectors 2–4 anchored in lowercity.

Fast Travel & Compass

/warp [name] opens the 54-slot Fast Travel District Menu; /waypoint [next|npcId|clear] locks the 3D HUD compass arrow; /lore and /timeline track the 25-node storyline.

RNG & Pity Meters

/rng [category] and /pity [category] display selected drop luck progression and deterministic milestones, fed via /rnghook and internal drop services.

Staff DCNPC Wands & Anchors

  • Setup Wand (/dcnpc wand): Places the 43 storyline characters + scenes and binds district warps.
  • Editor Wand (/dcnpc editor): Inspects stages, dialogue lines, yaw facing, and patrol waypoints; /setwarp <name> calibrates warp anchors.
