Profile GUI Layout (/profile [player])

Armor, Cosmetic Drawers & Summary

  • Column 1 (Slots 10, 19, 28, 37): Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, Boots (Main Hand 12, Offhand 21).
  • Column 0 Drawers (Slots 9, 18, 27, 36): Sliding drawers for head, chest, waist, and boots showing attached cosmetic scrolls.
  • Slot 13 (Player Head): Live Keystone tier/energy/heat, active Pet name & level, and stacked combat/utility stats (%dragoncote_stat_*%).
  • Slot 51 (tooltipToggle): Owner-only button displaying %dragoncote_tooltip_mode% to flip between COMPACT and EXPANDED item lore.

Column 2 Equipment Storage

  • Slot 11 (keystone): Temporal Keystone slot. Strictly accepts valid Keystones (Tesseract, Chronolith, Singularity); left-click opens the 54-slot KeystoneMenu.
  • Slot 20 (amulet): Amulet accessory slot (feeds StatSource.ACCESSORIES).
  • Slot 29 (cloak): Cloak accessory slot.
  • Slot 38 (gloves): Gloves accessory slot.

Cosmetic Engine, Wardrobe & Glow

Blockbench & Kinematics

  • Place .bbmodel / .json files in imports/ and run /cosmetics import <file> [slot].
  • left_ / right_ (Boots): inverse kinematic walking stride.
  • wing_left_ / wing_right_ (Chest): reactive glide/flap/sprint wings.
  • animated: true: supports spin, float, and glitch.

Combat VFX & Footsteps

  • Sword templates define attack_types: crit | sweep | regular rendered via ProtocolLib.
  • Boots footstep: configures custom particle, hex color, and sound (/cosmetics sound toggles local audio).
  • Procedural shapes: cube, solid, flat_ring, cylinder, star, burst, hourglass.

Wardrobe, Customise & /glow

  • /customise opens the dye & detach GUI for #FFFFFF colorable cosmetics.
  • /wardrobe studio configures V2 camera angles (overview, head, chest, waist, boots, teleport).
  • /glow <color|off> toggles scoreboard-team outline glow colors cleanly.
