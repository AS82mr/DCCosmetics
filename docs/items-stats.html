Unified Damage Pipeline (StatCombatEngine)

Both live combat and /dce tester dummies share the exact same resolution order inside StatCombatEngine:

Incoming Damage Resolution Order

  1. Unstable Keystone Vulnerability: At ≥ 80 Heat (without Singularity Anchor stabilization), emperor-tagged entities, SONIC_BOOM, and void causes deal +15% incoming damage.
  2. DEFLECT Glancing Parry: Rolls min(35%, X%) chance; on proc, halves incoming entity hit damage (-50%) and plays spark feedback.
  3. WARD / AEGIS Reduction: Applies WARD (max 60%) for physical/projectile hits, or AEGIS (max 60%) for hazard/magic causes.
  4. Combined Mitigation Floor: Total percentage reduction can never exceed 80%.
  5. BASTION Single-Hit Ceiling: While your HP is at or above 60% of max health, caps single-hit loss at maxHP × max(0.40, 1 - BASTION/100).

On-Kill Conduit Siphon & Transient Attributes

  • Conduit Mob-Kill Siphon: Killing a hostile mob grants min(25%, CONDUIT)% chance to siphon 25–100 mC into your Keystone buffer (rate-limited at 400 mC/min per player).
  • BASTION Poise Attribute: Applies transient KNOCKBACK_RESISTANCE (min(0.30, X/200), key dce_bastion_kb) on snapshot refresh.
  • TEMPO Haste Attribute: Applies transient BLOCK_BREAK_SPEED (+min(50%, X)%, key dce_tempo_break) on the main thread.

    Gathering & Artisan Bonuses (GatheringBonusService)

    PROSPERITY & Anti-Dupe Protection

    • Combines gear PROSPERITY + pet GATHERING_FORTUNE perks to duplicate actual dropped items via BlockDropItemEvent and PlayerFishEvent.
    • Backed by chunk-PDC PlacedBlockTracker on ores, crops, logs, melons, pumpkins, and sugar cane — player-placed blocks never proc bonus drops.
    • Silk Touch tools and Creative mode are strictly excluded.

    TEMPO & REFINEMENT

    • TEMPO (max 50%): Boosts mining break speed via Attribute.BLOCK_BREAK_SPEED and reduces fishing FishHook min/max wait ticks by X%.
    • REFINEMENT (max 40%): Awards +X% Skill XP on crafting, brewing, and enchanting, plus gives an X% chance to refund enchantment level costs cleanly.

    Unified Tooltips, Vanilla Enchant Caps & Socket Layout

    Option A Framing, Modes & Enchants (enchants.yml)

    All native DCE items and Scrolls share the Option A frame configured in DCEssentials/rarities.yml (top rarity/category glyphs + 1 gradient header, bottom 1 gradient footer, rarity-matched item names, and zero duplicate stars).

    • /tooltip [compact|expanded|toggle] — Saves persistent setting to dce_player_settings.
    • Vanilla Enchants & Live Sync (3 per line): Managed via DCEssentials/enchants.yml (vanilla enchantments only). Levels ≥ cap highlight in &c; lower levels render in &9. Enchants are tracked via live PDC signature (dce_ench_sig) so Grindstones (PrepareGrindstoneEvent), Anvils, Enchanting Tables, book drag-and-drop, /enchant//enchants//disenchant commands, inventory sync, and outgoing packets automatically reflect actual enchantments.
    • Reward Book Support: Over-cap vanilla books (e.g. sharpness: 6) apply via Anvil & drag-and-drop up to enchants.yml without unlocking over-cap levels in vanilla Enchanting Tables.
    • Tooltip Styles (tooltip_style): Configured per rarity in rarities.yml (with mastery mapped to lztooltips:artifact and matching mastery_*/artifact_* sprites under ItemsAdder/contents/lztooltips/resourcepack/assets/). Live items auto-heal mismatched tooltip styles on inventory refresh and outgoing packets.
    • Dynamic ↯ Costs: Ability costs written with {mc:...} dynamically display the viewer's Conduit/Keystone-discounted ↯ cost only inside their own inventory; drops, chests, AH, and trades show base cost.
    • /dce inspect — Read-only GUI displaying the full expanded breakdown of your held item.

    Void Warper & Stat Tester Dummy

    Void Warper (void_warper.yml)

    • Right-Click (Void Bounce · 75↯ base): Computes safe blink destination (up to 8.0m) before charging. If displacement is ≤ 0.05m, no energy is spent; otherwise spends 75↯ (reduced by Tier + Conduit + Flux, up to 70% off).
    • Shift + Right-Click (Sonic Boom · 250↯ base): Charges for 1.2s with Slowness V, cancels safely if the player dies mid-charge, then charges 250↯ on detonation for a 38.0m dash dealing 14.0 damage.
    • Overdraw Mode: At 0 Energy/↯ (or without an attuned Keystone), abilities remain castable by adding configured Overdraw Heat instead (+6 Bounce / +15 Sonic Boom).
