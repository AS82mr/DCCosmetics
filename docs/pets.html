Core Architecture & Stationary Perch Physics

Lv 1–100 & Perks (1/2/3/4/5)

All pet stats (including any of the 16 ItemStats via STAT_* perks) scale smoothly from Lv 1 to Lv 100. Perks unlock at milestones based on rarity: Common (1), Uncommon (2), Rare (3), Epic (4), Legendary & Mastery (5).

Smoothed Stationary Perch

  • Uses 0.06 / 0.03 horizontal delta hysteresis over 6 ticks to detect movement cleanly (supports walking, mounts, and Elytra).
  • Stationary: Freezes horizontal anchorXZ and shoulder yaw while keeping subtle vertical bobbing. Your pet stays put instead of whipping around when you turn your camera.
  • Idle Gaze & Re-Anchor: Smoothly turns up to 15°/tick toward your combat target or eyes while idle, and re-anchors if distance exceeds 3.2m or on world change.

VFX, Auto-Cast & Target Ring

Companions project a glowing particle ring under looked-at targets (/pet tracker), trigger active combat skills automatically (/pet autocast), and spawn clean dce_vfx_* ModelEngine animations.

SkillDomain & Weighted Adapt XP Mapping

Instead of flat skill lists that misclassify hybrid skills (such as Adapt axes earning both combat hit XP and woodcutting XP), PetsConfig maps every Adapt skill across 12 normalized SkillDomain weights (e.g., axes: {MELEE: 0.5, FORAGING: 0.5}, and mining scrolls fixed to pickaxe). Optional pets.exclude-scroll-bonus-xp prevents Mastery scroll self-grants from double-feeding pet XP.
