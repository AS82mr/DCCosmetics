Non-Destructive Swaps & Overflow Protection

Unequipping a Keystone never wipes your energy — it simply becomes "parked" until re-attuned. Downgrading to a lower tier or detaching a Capacitor scroll refunds any energy above the new capacity directly as physical tesseract_cube items.

The 1,000↯ Microcharge Buffer & Overdraw

How Cracking & Spending Works

  • Conversion Ratio: 1 whole Tesseract charge (🗲) equals 1,000 Microcharges (↯) inside your active buffer.
  • Ability Costs: Gameplay abilities spend Microcharges cleanly (e.g. Void Bounce = 75↯, Sonic Boom = 250↯). Whenever micro < cost and usableEnergy > 0, the matrix automatically cracks 1 🗲 into +1,000↯.
  • Full Warp Costs: Charged commands (/home, /tpa, /tpahere, /tpaccept, /tpyes) cost 1,000–3,000↯ through the exact same buffer, so leftover Microcharges are never wasted.
  • Free Bypass Commands: /back, /spawn, /warp, /pwarp, and /pw are validated accurately via Bukkit CommandMap and cost 0↯. Ender pearls are also exempt.
  • Cost Reduction Pool: Tier bonus + CONDUIT stat + Flux scrolls combine additively up to a hard 70% cost-reduction cap.

Thermodynamics, Unstable Heat & Overload

Unstable State (80–99 🔥)

  • Emits soul-fire sparks and warns nearby players within 10 blocks at 85+.
  • Applies safe spatial arrival jitter (2–4 blocks) on warps unless suppressed by an Epic/Legendary/Mastery Anchor scroll.
  • Applies +15% vulnerability against Emperor bosses, SONIC_BOOM, and Void damage.

Overload Lock (100 🔥)

  • When Heat hits 100, Overload triggers a 300s Nexus Incursion sentinel encounter.
  • While overloaded, charged warps and active Keystone abilities are denied until Heat cools to ≤ 50 🔥 (free commands like /spawn & /warp remain usable).

Cryo Cooling & Shields

  • Cryo Immersion: Submerging in Water or Powder Snow cools 20 🔥 every 5 seconds.
  • THERMAL Stat & Heatsinks: Reduce Heat accumulation by up to 60% and speed up passive 30s cooling cycles.
  • Newbie Shield: First 3 days grant 0 Heat on all Overdraw warps and abilities.

Native /keystone Matrix GUI & Multi-Socket Scrolls

54-Slot KeystoneMenu Controls

  • Slot 11: Attuned Keystone overview, effective capacity, and active scroll list.
  • Slots 13–15: Live Energy bar (🗲), Microcharge buffer bar (0–1,000↯), and Heat stability/Overload gauge.
  • Slots 20–22: Up to 3 Keystone Scroll socket slots (drop scroll from cursor to infuse, Shift-Click to detach & refund). Duplicate mechanic families are rejected.
  • Slots 28–34: Race-safe cube deposit (+1, +10, All, cursor deposit) and withdraw (-1, -10). Accepts both native tesseract_cube and legacy Energy Cubes.
  • Hotbar Shortcut: Right-click a Tesseract Energy Cube in hand to feed +1 🗲 (Shift+Right-Click feeds full stack up to capacity).

Keystone Scroll Families

  • KEYSTONE_HEAT (Heatsink / ks_thermal_*): Reduces warp/ability Overdraw Heat by 15%–35% and accelerates passive cooling by +25% to +60%.
  • KEYSTONE_TESSERACT (Flux / ks_flux_*): Reduces Microcharge cost by 10%–25% (shares 70% reduction cap).
  • KEYSTONE_CAPACITOR (ks_capacitor_*): Increases effective max energy capacity by +50 / +100 / +150 🗲 (never past 800).
  • KEYSTONE_ANCHOR (ks_anchor_*): Negates Nether 2.0x heat (Epic), suppresses unstable warp jitter (Legendary), and locks Heat at 99 to prevent Overload (Mastery).
  • KEYSTONE_RECLAIM (ks_reclaim_epic): Refunds 40% of charged /tpa costs directly back as Microcharges.
