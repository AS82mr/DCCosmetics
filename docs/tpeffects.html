Standalone Storage & Triggers

Rarity Organization

  • One File Per Rarity: uncommon.yml, rare.yml, epic.yml, legendary.yml, mastery.yml.
  • Per-Effect Timing: Optional duration: key (4s, 80t, 4000ms) scales phase and camera timings proportionally.
  • Live Toggle Commands: /tpeffect enable|disable [id] and /tpeffect hide|show <id>.

Persistence & Telemetry

  • Equipped effect and personal mute flag store directly in Player PDC (selected_tpeffect, muted_tpeffect).
  • Optional LuckPerms sync mirrors active choice to dc.seltpeffect.<id> and migrates legacy perms automatically on join.
  • TpEffectListener skips Wardrobe sessions, muted players, and short teleports, playing arrival plunges for Nether/End portals.

Wardrobe Interaction & Blackout Hooks

Hover → Preview → Equip Flow

  • Hover / Hotbar Scroll: Highlights card and stops any previous mannequin showcase cleanly.
  • 1st Left-Click: Plays MythicMob showcase on the mannequin NPC, or transitions to open-sky room.tp_preview for full 3D camera cinematics before returning with [ CLICK AGAIN TO EQUIP ].
  • 2nd Left-Click: Verifies ownership (dc.tpeffect.<id>) and equips or unequips.

Fullscreen Blackout & Camera Pipeline (Internal)

  • cinematic.blackout.mode supports AUTO, ULTIMATEUI_HUD, ULTIMATEUI_PANEL, TITLE_GLYPH, and POTION_DARKNESS.
  • Requires ItemsAdder pack contents/dragoncote_tp/ (tp_white.png + 21 items/tp_rig/*.json + 1,353 models/item/tp_px/*.json).
  • Uses interpolated TextDisplay camera entities with 32-bit float position sync packets and rayTraceBlocks collision checks.
